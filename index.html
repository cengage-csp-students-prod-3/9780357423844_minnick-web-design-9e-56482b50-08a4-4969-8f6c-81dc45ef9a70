🧩 Download Help

New to Sims 4 CC? We've got you!

1
Click the "Download" button next to each CC piece — it'll take you to the original creator's post or Patreon.
2
Download the .package file and place it in your Sims 4 Mods folder.
3
Make sure "Enable Custom Content and Mods" is turned on in your game settings.
4
Restart your game — and voilà, your new outfit is ready to wear!
